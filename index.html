Stromboli

Monday option · Sciara del Fuoco after dark

The Romans called Stromboli the Lighthouse of the Mediterranean around 300 BC and it has been in near-continuous eruption since. Activity and access restrictions change week to week, so check the official bulletins close to the day.

Mooring: reserved buoys at Marina del Gabbiano, 600 m north of Scari. Do not anchor — the bottom is very rocky. Guided summit excursions run 5–6 hours via MAGMATREK; the observatory hike is about half an hour.
