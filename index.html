Stromboli

Monday-night option · Sciara del Fuoco night sail

The Romans called Stromboli the Lighthouse of the Mediterranean around 300 BC and it has been in near-continuous eruption since. Volcanic activity changes week to week and access restrictions change with it, so check the official bulletins close to the day rather than relying on anything cached here.

Mooring: reserved buoys at Marina del Gabbiano, 600 m north of Scari. Do not anchor — the bottom is very rocky. Guided summit excursions run 5–6 hours through MAGMATREK; the observatory hike is about half an hour.
